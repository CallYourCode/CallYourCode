Pair your phone

cyc pair      # a Local/Cloud chooser, then a link (or QR) to open on your phone
cyc start     # start the services

The pairing key is the one secret, and it moves out of band: shown on the machine, entered on the phone by link, QR or typed, even on localhost. It travels in a URL fragment, which never reaches any server, log or Referer.

A device that cannot prove it holds the key gets a pairing screen, not a connection. A changed engine identity stops the app with an explicit decision, never auto-trust.

Voice models

About 1 GB of voice models download in the background on first boot; each voice capability switches on the moment its files land.
