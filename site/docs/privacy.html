Privacy model

The phone-to-engine channel is sealed end to end (AES-256-GCM, keys derived per connection per direction); a captured frame is garbage on the next connection.

The pairing key and every content key never touch the app server. The server forwards opaque strings and sealed blobs. It holds no key material and has no unsealed mode to fall back to.

Enrollment is key-gated on every transport, localhost included. Revoked devices are refused at the handshake and at the relay.

Voice is processed on the machine. No audio and no transcript ever leaves it.

The hosted path

Local and tailnet users need none of this. For everyone else the hosted server has three jobs, exactly: login, engine discovery, sealed push relay. The engine reaches out (no inbound ports), and once the sealed DataChannel opens the server is out of the path. It can die and open chats do not notice.
