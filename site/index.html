CallYourCode

Talk to your own coding agents from your phone, in your own voice.

$ curl -fsSL https://callyourcode.com/install.sh | sh

Your machine. Your tailnet. No company server in the path.

The wow moment

Walk away from the desk and your phone rings itself into a conversation: an agent finishes a task and speaks the result. You reply out loud, "run the tests again and tell me what broke," and the words land in the pane as input while the agent keeps going. The whole loop runs on your own hardware over your own network.

How it works

  1. Install

    Run the one-liner. It installs Bun if missing, clones the repo to ~/callyourcode, wires up your detected agents, and starts the services.

  2. Pair

    Run cyc pair, pick Local or Cloud, and open the link (or scan the QR) on your phone.

  3. Talk

    Open an agent pane on your phone and speak. What you say lands as ordinary terminal input; the agent speaks back.

  phone  <===  sealed E2E WebRTC  ===>  your machine
                                        (agent panes in tmux)

Works with

Claude Code / Codex / OpenCode / Pi

over tmux (the default) or herdr.

Private by construction

  • The phone-to-engine link is a sealed, end-to-end encrypted WebRTC DataChannel. No company server sits in the data path.
  • Speech is synthesized and transcribed in-process by sherpa-onnx (kokoro for text to speech, whisper for speech to text). No cloud speech API, and no audio leaves your machine.
  • Everything is four Bun/TypeScript services and a PWA, running on your own hardware.
